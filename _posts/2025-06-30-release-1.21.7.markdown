A hot-as-lava hotfix. It's Minecraft 1.21.7, bringing updates to your disco, plus a fresco and some crash and bug fixes. We've added a new 3x3 painting to honor everyone's best friend, as well as refreshed the loot dropped by an iconic mob. The Baby Zombie riding a Chicken (Chicken Jockey) will drop the “Lava Chicken” music disc when defeated.<br>

We will be back with the next snapshot in a few weeks.<br>

Happy Mining!<br>

**Changelog:**<br>
\* Added a painting of a good boy by artist Sarah Boeving<br>
\* Added music disc "Lava Chicken" by Hyper Potions<br>
